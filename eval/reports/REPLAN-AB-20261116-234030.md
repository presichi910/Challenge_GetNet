## How to read the differences

A difference on a row where the replan did not fire cannot be attributed to the replan: on that row the observe step took no action, so the answer came from the same steps as in the baseline, and a different verdict comes from the run-to-run variation of the agents or of the judge. Even on a row where the replan fired, one repetition cannot separate the replan from that variation.
